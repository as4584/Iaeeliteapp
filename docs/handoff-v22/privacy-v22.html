Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

IAE Elite Services (Innovation Artist Escort), operated by IBDS Enterprise LLC ("IAE," "we," "us"), respects your privacy. This policy explains what this app does and does not collect.

What we collect

Only what you choose to send us. If you complete the Request form, you may provide your name, phone number, email address, organization, event date, city, the service you want, and any details you type. The app does not store this information on a server of ours. When you tap Send, the app opens your device's email app with a pre-filled message addressed to us; the message leaves your device only when you press Send in that email app, and we receive it by email.

The app has no accounts or sign-in, and it does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking tools. It does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or other device data.

Calls, FaceTime, WhatsApp and email

The Call Now, Email, FaceTime and WhatsApp buttons open your phone, mail, FaceTime or WhatsApp apps. Those apps and services handle the communication under their own terms and privacy policies. When you contact us this way, we may see your phone number, Apple ID, WhatsApp number or profile name, and the content of your message.

How we use it

We use the information you send to respond to you, evaluate and plan your request, coordinate and confirm services, and keep business and legal records. We do not sell your information and we do not share it for advertising. We share it only with the personnel and partners needed to carry out an engagement you confirm, and where the law requires.

Non-disclosure is standard on our engagements. We treat principal details, schedules and locations as confidential.

Third parties

The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google may receive your IP address and device information when the app loads. The app also fetches a small settings file (our current phone number, email and links) from our own website; that request reaches our host like any web request, and carries nothing you entered. The app also links to Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, and our websites (iaeelite.com and innovationdevelopmentsolutions.com). Those services are not controlled by us, and their own policies apply once you leave the app.

Retention

We keep your request for as long as needed to respond to you, carry out any engagement, and meet business and legal record-keeping needs.

Your choices

You may ask us to tell you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it by emailing innovationbusiness@icloud.com. We will respond within a reasonable time and as the law requires. Residents of some states and countries have additional rights under local law, and we honor valid requests.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information we receive. Email is not guaranteed to be secure, so please do not send Social Security numbers, payment card numbers, passwords, or similar sensitive data through the Request form.

Children

This app is intended for adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version, and the version in the app is the current one.
